# Some things to look at

Not required. Not graded. These are here because they are good, and
because they disagree with each other in useful ways.

---

### Don't trust the design process
**Jenny Wen** · essay
<https://jennywen.ca/notes/dont-trust-the-design-process>

Short. Read it early, because we are about to teach you a process and you should hold it loosely from the start. A process is scaffolding for when you are stuck, not a guarantee that anything good comes out the other end.

---

### The design process is dead. Here’s what’s replacing it.
**Jenny Wen** · video
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eh8bcBIAAFo>

The same argument as her essay, made out loud and taken further. Watch it after the essay, not before.

---

### Design Engineering at Vercel
**Glenn Hitchcock and others** · post · optional
<https://vercel.com/blog/design-engineering-at-vercel>

What the third column on the three jobs slide looks like at a company that takes it seriously.

### Explain it like I’m 5: What is a Product Designer?
**Henry Wu** · essay
<https://medium.com/hubspot-product/explain-it-like-im-5-what-is-a-product-designer-121aad98c047>

The grocery store explanation we go through in class.

---

### Product design in 2026: the beginning of a fantastic voyage?
**Kike Peña** · essay
<https://uxdesign.cc/product-design-in-2026-the-beginning-of-a-fantastic-voyage-fb6866c907ac>

We go through this in class. Read it again afterwards, once you have something to argue with.

---

### IDEO Shopping Cart
**ABC Nightline** · video
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M66ZU2PCIcM>

Eight minutes, filmed in 1999. A design team is given five days to redesign the shopping cart. Watch how much of it is arguing and watching people, and how little is drawing.

---

### The Design of Everyday Things, ch. 1
**Don Norman** · book chapter

About 25 pages, in the course packet. The doors you push when you should pull. Read it once, quickly, and do not take notes.

---

### Objectified
**Gary Hustwit** · film · optional

Feature length. Industrial designers talking about the objects around them. Good background for the whole semester, not homework.
